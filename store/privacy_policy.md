# Privacy Policy — QuestHabit

**Effective date:** 9 October 2026
**Developer contact:** ravi9689370-oss@users.noreply.github.com

QuestHabit ("the App") is an offline-first habit tracker game. This policy explains what data the App collects and how it is handled.

## 1. Data we collect

The App collects **only data you create inside the app**, stored locally on your device:

- Your hero profile (display name, class, color choice)
- Habits you add (titles, types, schedules, difficulty)
- Habit completion logs and workout history
- Game progress (XP, level, gold, items, quests, achievements)
- App settings (theme, language, notification preference)

All of this is stored in your device's local storage (IndexedDB / localStorage). **No account is created. No personal identifiers (email, phone, device ID, advertising ID) are collected.**

## 2. Data we do NOT collect

- No location data
- No contacts, camera, microphone, or photos
- No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs are included
- No third-party trackers

## 3. Advertising & Purchases

- **Ads (optional, Android only):** The App may show rewarded advertisements via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect data per Google's policies (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). Rewarded ads are opt-in: you choose to watch them for in-game rewards.
- **In-app purchases (Android only):** Google Play Billing processes Premium purchases. Payment data is handled by Google, not by the App.

## 4. Data sharing

We share nothing. Data never leaves your device except:
- Payment processing via Google Play Billing (purchase tokens)
- Ad requests via AdMob when you opt in to a rewarded ad

## 5. Data retention & deletion

All data is stored on your device and deleted when you uninstall the App. You can delete everything at any time via **Settings → Delete all data**, which wipes the App's local storage immediately and permanently.

## 6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

## 7. Changes

We may update this policy in future releases; the effective date above will change accordingly.
